HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Survive as long as possible while dodging enemies and collecting coins!

CONTROLS

Desktop: Arrow Keys or A/D to move, Spacebar to shoot

Mobile: Use the on-screen buttons to move and shoot

GAMEPLAY

• Dodge enemy projectiles and collisions

• Shoot enemies to destroy them and earn points

• Collect coins for currency and bonuses

• Use power-ups strategically

• Build combo multipliers for higher scores

• Defeat bosses at certain levels

ENEMIES

Different enemy types have different behaviors. Learn their patterns!

PROGRESSION

Earn coins to unlock ships and upgrades. Complete missions for bonus rewards!
